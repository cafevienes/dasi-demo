09 — Visual Direction

Photography & Imagery

影像使用規範

Photographic Identity
  • Tone & Mood: Deep, cinematic. Low-key lighting preferred. Dramatic shadows. Rich blacks. Images should feel curated, not stock.
  • Subject Matter: Architecture (glass towers, night skylines), boardroom environments, formal handshakes, data visualizations, maps. Abstract textures in gold/navy.
  • Color Treatment: Desaturate by 20–30%. Apply a warm gold color grade. Never use heavily saturated photography. No filters that clash with navy/gold palette.
  • Composition: Prefer rule-of-thirds with significant negative space. Allow text overlay with gradient veil (transparent → #0C1024 at 70%).
  • People: Executive portraiture only. Professional attire, neutral backgrounds or architecture. Cropped at chest or above. Never casual or lifestyle.
  • Prohibited: Stock photo stereotypes, overly happy posed imagery, bright saturated colors, lens flares, tilt-shift effects.

10 — Brand Identity

Logo Usage Rules

標誌使用規範

DASI
Primary: Gold on Deep Background. Standard usage on all dark-themed digital and print materials.
DASI
Reverse: White on Brand Indigo. Use for presentations, printed covers, accent panels.
DASI
Light: Brand Indigo on Warm White. Use on light backgrounds, stationery, reports.
DASI
Special: Deep color on Gold. Use sparingly — awards, certificates, premium print only.
Minimum Clear Space
DASI
Clear space = cap-height of logotype on all four sides. Never crowd with other elements.
Never Do This
  • Stretch or distort the logo proportions
  • Apply drop shadows or emboss effects
  • Use on busy photographic backgrounds without overlay
  • Outline or stroke the logotype
  • Recreate the logo in unauthorized typefaces
  • Place on colors not in the DASI palette
  • Reduce below 60px width in digital contexts

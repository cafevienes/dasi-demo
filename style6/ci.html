10 — Visual Direction

Photography & Imagery

影像使用規範

Photographic Identity
  • Tone & Mood: Deep, cinematic on dark canvas. Professional and forward-looking on indigo sections. Rich contrast across both contexts.
  • Subject Matter: Architecture (glass towers, night skylines), boardroom environments, professional portraits, abstract textures. Modern Taipei cityscape for heroes.
  • Color Treatment: Brand Indigo overlay (60-80% opacity, multiply) on dark sections. Reduced saturation (40-60%) on indigo hero blocks.
  • Icons: Outlined style (Phosphor/Lucide), 24px, stroke 1.5px. Gold on dark background. White on indigo background.
  • Prohibited: Saturated stock photos, cartoon icons, clip art, busy backgrounds, lens flares.

11 — Brand Identity

Logo Usage Rules

標誌使用規範

DASI
Primary: Gold on Deep Background. Standard usage on dark-themed pages.
DASI
On Brand Indigo: White logotype + Gold accents. Hero sections, nav, footer.
DASI
Light mode: Brand Indigo on Warm White. Print, stationery, reports.
DASI
Special: Deep color on Gold. Awards, certificates, premium print only.
Never Do This
  • Stretch or distort the logo proportions
  • Apply drop shadows or emboss effects
  • Use on busy photographic backgrounds without overlay
  • Outline or stroke the logotype
  • Recreate the logo in unauthorized typefaces
  • Place on colors not in the DASI palette
  • Reduce below 60px width in digital contexts

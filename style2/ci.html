Color Philosophy

No gradients. No shadows. No colored backgrounds beyond this palette. The constraint is the principle. Whitespace is the dominant design element. Color is reserved for hierarchy and action, never decoration.

08 — Animation

Motion Specs

動態規範

Page Entrance
opacity: 0 → 1 · 0.8s · ease · no movement
All content enters via opacity fade only. No translateY. No slide. Barely perceptible — the goal is that it feels like the page is simply being revealed.
Interactive Color
color / border-color · 0.25s · ease
Standard transition for all hover color changes. Link colors, button backgrounds, border weights. Applied on base state, never on :hover selector.
Border Weight
border-bottom-width: 1px → 2px · 0.25s
Secondary button hover state. Subtle weight change communicates interaction without visual noise.
Guiding Principle
Stillness · Precision · Invisibility
Motion should never be noticed as motion. If a user comments on the animation, it is too much. The design philosophy is editorial stillness — content moves ideas, not pixels.
09 — Visual Direction

Photography & Imagery

影像使用規範

Editorial Image Direction
  • Tone: Restrained, high-contrast, desaturated. Print-quality aesthetic. Images should feel like editorial photography from a serious financial publication.
  • Subject Matter: Architectural exteriors, empty boardrooms, hands on paper/documents, city skylines from above. Economic geography: ports, infrastructure, cityscapes.
  • Color Treatment: Convert to warm monochrome or near-monochrome. No vibrant colors. Images should look like they belong in a cream-toned layout without fighting it.
  • Composition: Generous negative space on at least two sides. Allow text to breathe alongside or over image. Never fill entire width with image without text breathing room.
  • People: Executive-only. Shot from distance or cropped at shoulder. Thoughtful pose — reading, writing, conversing. No forced smiles or staged diversity photography.
  • Scale: Prefer large single images to grids of small images. One exceptional photograph over four mediocre ones.
  • Prohibited: Gradients over images, colorful filters, stock pose photography, icons overlaid on images, text directly on unobscured imagery.

10 — Brand Identity

Logo Usage Rules

標誌使用規範

DASI
Primary: Brand Indigo on Paper White. Default for all editorial and print materials.
DASI
On Cream: Brand Indigo on Cream. Cards, panel headers, callout boxes.
DASI
Reversed: White on Brand Indigo. Report covers, presentation headers.
DASI
On White: Charcoal on pure white. Digital environments, web interfaces.
Clearspace Rule
DASI
Minimum clear space equals one x-height of the logotype on all four sides.
Never Do This
  • Distort or stretch proportions
  • Apply shadows or texture effects
  • Use unauthorized typefaces to recreate
  • Place on pattern or photographic backgrounds
  • Change approved color combinations
  • Reduce below 56px in digital contexts
  • Outline or stroke the letterforms

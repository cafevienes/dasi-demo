09 — Animation & Motion

Motion Specs

動態規範

Page Entrance
fadeInUp · 0.5s · ease · both
Elements enter from 20px below opacity 0. Shorter duration than other styles — more direct and businesslike. Stagger: 0.08s.
Card Hover Lift
translateY(-4px) · 0.25s · ease
Cards lift 4px on hover — more pronounced than other styles, reinforcing interactivity. Paired with shadow increase and indigo border.
Counter Animation
counter 0 → N · 1.5s · easeOut
Statistical numbers animate from 0 on scroll entry. Creates confidence and draws attention to key metrics. Implement via IntersectionObserver + JS.
Button Lift
translateY(-1px) · 0.2s · ease
Buttons lift subtly on hover. Primary adds shadow intensity. All transitions defined on base state — never on :hover pseudo-class.
Nav Underline
width: 0 → 100% · 0.2s · ease
Crimson underline extends under nav links on hover. Fast and precise — signals interactivity without delay.
Focus Ring
box-shadow expand · 0.15s · ease
3px Indigo Tint ring appears on input focus. Accessibility compliant. Replaces browser default outline. Border transitions to Brand Indigo simultaneously.
10 — Visual Direction

Photography & Imagery

影像使用規範

Corporate Photography Direction
  • Tone: Professional, authoritative, optimistic. Well-lit boardrooms, confident executive portraiture, institutional architecture. Never dark or moody.
  • Subject Matter: Glass office buildings, city skylines, handshakes and meetings, digital interfaces/screens, financial charts, Asian business hubs (Singapore, Hong Kong, Taipei, Shanghai).
  • Color Treatment: Slightly desaturated (10–15%). Can add a cool, slightly blue-shifted grade to harmonize with indigo palette. Avoid warm yellows or oranges.
  • Composition: Clean, open compositions. Large sky areas for text overlay. Subject in one-third of frame. Never cropped awkwardly at edges.
  • People: Diverse executive portraiture. Confident posture. Professional attire. Can use candid shots of team in action (meetings, whiteboarding). Always well-lit.
  • On Indigo Backgrounds: Use overlay images with Brand Indigo at 70% opacity + multiply blend. Creates unified section treatment.
  • Prohibited: Low-resolution imagery, clichéd stock (handshake close-ups, generic globes), overly casual photography, images with competing strong colors.

11 — Brand Identity

Logo Usage Rules

標誌使用規範

DASI
Primary: Brand Indigo on White. Default for all digital and light-background contexts.
DASI
On Off White: Brand Indigo on Off White. Reports, presentations, gray panels.
DASI
Reversed: White on Brand Indigo. All dark/colored backgrounds, nav bar, dark headers.
DASI
On Crimson: White on Brand Crimson. Avoid — use only where no other option exists.
Clearspace Rule
DASI
Minimum clear space = cap-height of logotype on all sides. Dashed border shows exclusion zone.
Never Do This
  • Stretch or distort proportions
  • Apply drop shadows, gradients, or glows
  • Place on busy photographic backgrounds
  • Recreate in unauthorized typefaces
  • Use colors outside approved palette
  • Reduce below 60px in digital contexts
  • Outline or stroke the letterforms
  • Apply opacity or transparency to logotype
